================================================================================
 READY CONVERTED FIRMWARE FILES FOR ANDROID (ZERO PC REQUIRED)
================================================================================

These files are fully converted HP download (.dl) binary streams.
They already contain the PJL/ACL printer header and are 100% ready to be
accepted directly by the printer over USB.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHICH FILE TO USE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- For HP LaserJet 1020 / 1020 Plus:
  --> Use: sihp1020.dl  (or firmware-1020.dl)
  (Size: 128,862 bytes)

- For HP LaserJet P1007:
  --> Use: sihpP1007.dl (or firmware-P1005.dl)
  (Size: 223,529 bytes)

- For HP LaserJet P1005 / P1006:
  --> Use: sihpP1005.dl / sihpP1006.dl

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO LOAD INTO THE ANDROID APP (PrintBridge Pro):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy or download the .dl file onto your Android phone (e.g. into your phone's
   "Download" folder).
2. Connect your HP printer to the Android phone using a USB OTG cable.
3. Turn on the printer.
4. Open the PrintBridge Pro app on the phone.
5. Tap the "Load Firmware" button.
6. Select the .dl file (e.g., sihp1020.dl for HP 1020 Plus).
7. The app sends the firmware to the printer immediately!
   You will hear the printer's rollers whir and initialize.
8. The app permanently saves the firmware in its internal storage!
   Next time you plug in the printer or open the app, it will automatically
   recognize the printer and send the firmware itself.
